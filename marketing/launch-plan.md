# Southern Sun Fishing Co. — Launch, SEO & Paid Ads Plan

This file is prep only. It does not create or launch ad accounts, spend money, change Shopify, or publish anything.

## 1. Goal

Use the finished storefront as the conversion destination, then build a simple growth loop around:

1. high-intent product traffic,
2. creative testing,
3. retargeting,
4. email capture,
5. weekly optimization.

Primary commercial categories:
- Hats
- T-Shirts
- Hooded / performance shirts

Primary brand angle:
- Southern coastal / backwater fishing lifestyle
- Fishing-first apparel rather than generic outdoor fashion
- Premium but approachable editorial look

## 3. Paid acquisition structure

### Phase 1 — Creative test
Start with three product angles:

A. Snook Hat / headwear
B. Mangrove or Skiff Tee
C. Hooded Performance Shirt

For each angle, make 3-5 creative variants:
- clean product image
- lifestyle image
- fish / fishing image with product tie-in
- editorial campaign layout
- simple offer / product-focused creative

Do not launch all creative with different targeting at once. Keep targeting stable so creative performance is easier to compare.

### Phase 2 — Winners
Once a creative/product pair clearly outperforms the others:
- keep the winner live,
- make 2-4 variations of the same winning concept,
- change one variable at a time: hook, crop, headline, product color, CTA.

### Phase 3 — Retargeting
Build a separate retargeting audience for:
- site visitors,
- product viewers,
- add-to-cart users,
- Instagram engagers,
- email subscribers.

Exclude purchasers where possible.

## 5. Landing-page strategy

Avoid sending every ad to the homepage.

Hat ads -> Hats section / collection
Tee ads -> T-Shirts section / collection
Performance ads -> Hoodies / performance section
Specific product ads -> specific product page

Keep message continuity:
If the ad says “Snook Hat,” the first screen after the click should show the Snook Hat.

## 6. Measurement framework

Track at minimum:
- Spend
- Impressions
- CPM
- Link clicks
- CTR
- CPC
- Product views
- Add to carts
- Checkout starts
- Purchases
- CPA
- Conversion rate
- Revenue
- ROAS

Do not scale from CTR alone. Sales and contribution margin matter more.

## 7. Weekly optimization routine

Every 7 days:
1. rank creatives by purchases / CPA / ROAS,
2. identify low-CTR or high-CPC creative,
3. pause obvious losers only after enough data exists,
4. preserve winning ads,
5. create new variants based on winners,
6. check landing-page drop-off,
7. review product-level conversion rate,
8. move budget toward proven product/creative combinations.
