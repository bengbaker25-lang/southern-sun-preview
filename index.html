ABOUT US

MADE TO LIVE
ON THE WATER.

Founded and run in Atlanta, Georgia, Southern Sun Fishing Co. is built on a love for life on the water and the drive to create something worth wearing. Fishing is where our inspiration starts. The clothing, the artwork, and every detail that carries our name are how we share it.

We put that passion into creating apparel with purpose: performance for long hours under the sun, comfort you want to live in, and artwork that feels personal. From the feel of a fabric to the smallest detail in a design, our goal is to make every piece a favorite—the shirt you reach for first, the hat that goes on every trip.

Our designs carry the places and experiences that mean the most to us. Quiet stretches of marsh. The run offshore at first light. A day that ends around the grill with friends, still talking about what happened on the boat. We want you to recognize a little of your own life in what we create.

Southern Sun is for the people who carry that feeling with them, wherever they are. As we grow, our commitment stays the same: keep creating with care, keep making better products, and give you something you’re proud to be part of.
